Overview.

The celestial plushie at the heart of the meme swarm.

Musegod is the mascot of swarm memes. Its token, $MUSEGOD, lives on Robinhood Chain and pairs with IMD in a Uniswap v4 pool.

A plush with a point of view.

Musegod inspires, judges, and gently roasts every meme trending on Robinhood Chain. Unflappable, incorruptible, and a little squishy.

No promises, no convoluted roadmaps. Just pure, shareable chaos, judged worthy or not by Musegod’s all-seeing plushness.

What this website does.

It reads public launch metadata, displays available market data, and lets you share or download memes. It has no wallet connector, transaction signing, trackers or data-collection forms.
