Tokenomics.

1,000,000,000 MUSEGOD. Fixed forever.

90% pool. 10% swarm.

The constructor mints the entire supply to its deployer. The factory handles the launch distribution: 900,000,000 tokens are the pool-seeding budget and 100,000,000 tokens go through the swarm’s Merkle distributor. No token contract sends or reserves the swarm allocation.

Liquidity math can leave a small remainder, forwarded by the factory to 0x000000000000000000000000000000000000dead. This is a transfer, not a reduction in total supply.

2% buy fee.

A fixed 2% token fee applies to transfers from the PoolManager, including liquidity withdrawals. It goes directly to the fixed treasury, rounded down to the nearest minor unit. Sells and ordinary transfers have a 0% token fee. The Uniswap v4 LP fee is a separate 1.25%.

No adjustable parameters.

18 decimals. No owner, minting, burning, upgradeability or fee setter. The entire supply remains 1e27 minor units.
